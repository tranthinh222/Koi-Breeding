Koi - Kohaku

Koi Kohaku are the most common and easily recognizable Koi variety, featuring a white body adorned with red (Hi) markings covering about 50–70% of the skin.

The brighter and larger the red patterns, the more valuable the fish, while the white base (Shiroji) must remain pure and radiant like snow.
